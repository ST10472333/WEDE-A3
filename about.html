OUR STORY

Born in Soweto.
Inspired by creativity.

Ntozinhle Lifestyle is an African-inspired fashion, accessories and homeware brand based in Soweto, South Africa. Its identity brings contemporary style together with influences from South African craft and culture.

The brand describes its products as handmade in South Africa, using materials such as beads and leather. Beadwork and design are important parts of its story, connecting traditional craft influences with pieces made for modern everyday life.

Its brand story also highlights social development and employment as part of its wider purpose.
